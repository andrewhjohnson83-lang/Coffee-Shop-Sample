Who We Are

Rooted in Jersey.
Connected to the World.

Garden State Coffee started as a weekend obsession in a Haddonfield kitchen. We were tired of driving across the bridge to Philly for a truly great cup — so we decided to bring that level of craft right here to New Jersey.

We’re obsessed with the full journey: from the terroir of a highland farm to the pour angle on a cortado. Every decision — from grind size to compostable cups — is intentional.

We’re not trying to be Starbucks. We’re not trying to be precious, either. We want to be your neighborhood spot that just happens to serve exceptional coffee.

Zero Waste Program

Compostable packaging, in-store collection bins, and a partnership with local farms to repurpose coffee grounds as soil amendment. Zero waste target: 2027.

Digital Loyalty

Our mobile app tracks your orders and rewards loyalty with free drinks, early access to seasonal releases, and behind-the-scenes content from our roasting floor.

Local Art Rotation

Every six weeks, a new local artist’s work fills the space. Pieces are available for purchase, with 20% going directly back to the artist.
